# Metallocene numeric extraction status

The A7 Cp'2Co numeric result is retained as an audit artifact but **downgraded from P1 to provisional P0-Q**.

Reason: the accessible OCR representation of Table II drops blank table cells in sparse rows. This makes column assignment ambiguous for some solvents even though the published qualitative trend is clear. The earlier extracted series may therefore contain column-assignment error.

Do not use the numeric Cp'2Co or HMFc pilot calculations as evidence until a visually aligned or otherwise unambiguous copy of Table II is recovered.

The source-level qualitative result remains valid: the paper reports strong solvent-dynamical control for the most facile cobaltocene system and progressively weaker dependence for less facile systems, with little clear dependence for the least facile ferrocene cases.

No manuscript promotion is allowed from A7 at this state.
