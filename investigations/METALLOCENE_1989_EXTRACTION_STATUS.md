# McManis et al. 1989 metallocene candidate status

**Status:** P0-D HIGH-PRIORITY LEAD / QUANTITATIVE TEST BLOCKED ON TABLE EXTRACTION

Primary paper:
McManis, Nielson, Gochev & Weaver, *Solvent Dynamical Effects in Electron Transfer: Evaluation of Electronic Matrix Coupling Elements for Metallocene Self-Exchange Reactions*, JACS 111 (1989) 5533-5541, DOI 10.1021/ja00197a004.

## Why it is scientifically eligible in principle

The paper reports:
- experimental self-exchange rates for six metallocene redox couples;
- independently determined solvent-dependent barriers;
- barrier-corrected rates;
- independently sourced longitudinal solvent relaxation times;
- 11 approximately Debye solvents plus several explicitly non-Debye solvents.

The paper's own analysis finds that the most facile cobaltocene couples retain strong solvent-dynamical dependence after barrier correction, while the least facile ferrocene systems become much less sensitive to solvent relaxation. This is highly relevant to a Capital-Chi rate-regime classifier.
