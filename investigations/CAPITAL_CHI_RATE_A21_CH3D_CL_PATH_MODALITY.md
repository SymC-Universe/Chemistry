# A21: CH3D + Cl path-dependent modal specificity

**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Status:** INDEPENDENT EMPIRICAL LITERATURE EVIDENCE; NUMERIC MODAL PATH NOT AVAILABLE

## Primary source

Yoon, Holiday, Sibert, and Crim, *The relative reactivity of CH3D molecules with excited symmetric and antisymmetric stretching vibrations*, J. Chem. Phys. 2003, 119, 9568-9575. DOI 10.1063/1.1615755.

Related source:
Yoon, Holiday, and Crim, *Vibrationally Controlled Chemistry: Mode- and Bond-Selected Reaction of CH3D with Cl*, J. Phys. Chem. B 2005, DOI 10.1021/jp0463565.

## Empirical contrast

Direct IR preparation near 3000 cm^-1 creates CH3D vibrational eigenstates containing different symmetric C-H stretch (nu1), antisymmetric C-H stretch (nu4), and bend-overtone character.

The source reports that:
- symmetric C-H stretch nu1 promotes H abstraction about seven times more efficiently than antisymmetric C-H stretch nu4;
- the two modes lie within roughly 80 cm^-1 of the same excitation energy in the related experimental summary.

Thus a scalar excitation-energy description is insufficient.

## Modal-path interpretation

The source's ab initio analysis follows vibrational energies/eigenvectors as the reactive Cl atom approaches.

It reports qualitatively different carrier evolution:

- initial nu1 character becomes localized vibrational excitation of the C-H bond pointing toward the approaching Cl atom;
- energy initially in nu4 flows into C-H bonds pointing away from the approaching Cl atom and remains comparatively spectator-like.

This identifies a modal correspondence/path object, not merely a reactant-minimum frequency.

A suitable Capital-Chi representation is therefore allowed to carry:

[
R_i(s): Q_i(s_0) \rightarrow Q_i(s)
]

or an overlap/transport map such as

[
O_{ij}(s)=|Q_i(s_0)^T M(s)Q_j(s)|
]

when a physically justified coordinate/metric correspondence is available.

The exact form must be frozen for a specific native model and is **not** inferred from the observed rate.

## Evidence boundary

The accessible publication record establishes:
- near-energy-matched modal preparation;
- strong measured mode specificity;
- qualitative ab initio eigenvector evolution explaining the difference.

The exact numerical eigenvector-overlap trajectory is not available in the accessible source material here. Therefore A21 is retained as independent empirical modal evidence, not converted into a quantitative Capital-Chi regression.

## Consequence

Capital Chi in chemistry should support both:

1. **static modal geometry** at a declared condition/configuration;
2. **modal correspondence/evolution** between configurations or along a reaction path.

Lowercase chi_i remains a scalar property of an admitted mode where its mechanical reduction exists. It does not encode this path-dependent change of modal identity.
