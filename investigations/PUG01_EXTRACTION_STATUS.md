# Pugzlys 2001 R800/DMA candidate status

**Status:** P0-D HIGH-RELEVANCE / QUANTITATIVE EXTRACTION BLOCKED

Primary paper:
Pugzlys et al., *Solvent-Controlled Acceleration of Electron Transfer in Binary Mixtures*, J. Phys. Chem. A 105 (2001) 11407-11413, DOI 10.1021/jp0123738.

## Scientific relevance

The source reports a roughly fivefold acceleration of backward electron transfer between Rhodamine 800 and N,N-dimethylaniline in DMA/acetonitrile mixtures and explicitly reports a linear dependence of electron-transfer time on the longitudinal solvent-relaxation time.

This is strongly aligned with the Capital-Chi environment-dynamics question.

## Extraction blocker

The exact mixture-by-mixture reaction-time and longitudinal-relaxation table is not exposed in the accessible abstract/metadata routes. The publisher full text is access restricted, the institutional repository exposes only a request-copy record, and no independent open copy with exact tabulated values was recovered.

Per the investigation firewall:
- no graph digitization is substituted for exact tabulated data;
- no values are inferred from the reported linear trend;
- no calculation is performed.

Candidate remains a high-priority exact-data lead if a lawful full text becomes available.
