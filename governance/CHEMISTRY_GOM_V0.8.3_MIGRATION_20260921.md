## v0.8.3 meaning for Chemistry

Chemistry already distinguishes local coordinates, modal/carrier structure, and system/embedded organization. v0.8.3 now requires their joint interpretation to be explicit where applicable.

A local scalar such as a mechanically licensed chi or a barrier friction coordinate does not define the broader chemical-system Chi architecture. Conversely, a network, surface, solvent, substrate, or coupled-system organization does not retroactively license a local scalar.

Where both are available, record:
- the local coordinate and its native generator;
- its modal/carrier lineage;
- relevant coupling/environment/substrate organization;
- what system organization changes about the realized local behavior;
- what the scalar loses relative to the richer description;
- refusal/non-identifiability when no coherent scalar exists.

## Perturbation/recovery

Do not redefine chemical stability as recovery.

Where a promoted claim genuinely concerns return, resilience, adaptation, or post-perturbation organization, distinguish resistance, finite-time response, first reclaim, sustained recovery, reorganization, basin robustness, and repeated-perturbation behavior.

Barrier crossing, relaxation, adsorption, reaction rate, surface reconstruction, and recovery are not synonyms. Each keeps its native physical definition.
